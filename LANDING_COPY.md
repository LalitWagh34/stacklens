## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no login, no catch. We show you the audit results
instantly. Email is optional and only asked after you see your results.

**Q: How do you know the pricing data is accurate?**
A: Every number in our audit engine is sourced from the official pricing page
of each vendor, verified weekly. Sources are documented in our public
PRICING_DATA.md on GitHub. If you find an error, email us and we'll fix it
within 24 hours.

**Q: Does this work for individual developers or only teams?**
A: Both. If you're a solo developer paying for multiple AI tools,
the audit will show you which ones overlap and which to keep.
Team features become more relevant at 3+ people.

**Q: What do you do with my data?**
A: Your tool names and savings numbers are stored to generate your shareable
audit URL. Your email is stored only if you choose to enter it.
We never sell your data. The public version of your audit URL strips
your email and company name — only tool names and savings numbers are shown.

**Q: What if the tool says I'm already spending optimally?**
A: We'll tell you honestly. "You're spending well" is a real output —
we don't manufacture fake savings to look useful. If you're already
optimized, we'll notify you when new savings opportunities apply to
your specific stack.
